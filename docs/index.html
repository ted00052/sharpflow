Alert rules
Data: Polymarket public APIs (Gamma · CLOB · Data-API), no auth, refreshed every 60s while open (whales 30s). Prices are implied probabilities in cents. Whale flow = taker trades ≥ your $ filter; net flow = buys − sells per outcome.
Edge model: sportsbook moneylines (ESPN/DraftKings free feed; optionally multi-book consensus via your the-odds-api key) converted to implied probability and de-vigged (normalized so both sides sum to 100%) — the standard "fair odds" benchmark, the same one Sportstensor scores its miners against. Edge = fair prob − Polymarket ask. O/U markets match only when the book line is identical. Caveats: a single book is a noisy oracle; ESPN odds exist pre-game only; a positive edge vs. the books is not a guaranteed profit — books move, and Polymarket sometimes leads them.
