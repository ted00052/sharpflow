SharpFlow — User Guide

Whale flow, price alerts, and a sportsbook fair-odds edge model for Polymarket sports markets.

1 · Open it in live mode

1Check the status dot (top right). ● LIVE means data is streaming — markets refresh every 60s, whale trades every 30s, book odds every 5 min, all while the page is open.
2If you see ● SNAPSHOT MODE and a yellow banner, the viewer you're in blocks external data. Fix: download the HTML file and double-click it so it opens in Chrome or Safari. From your browser everything streams live — no server, no login, no keys.
3Leave the tab open during game windows. Auto-refresh pauses when the tab is hidden and resumes when you come back; the ↻ Refresh button forces an update any time.

2 · The 5-minute daily routine

  1. Edge tab first. Anything above your edge threshold? (Most days: no. That's normal and correct.)
  2. Whale Feed. Scan the consensus table — where is big money net-concentrating today?
  3. Cross-check. A market that shows a book edge and whale flow on the same side is the only signal worth real attention.
  4. Chart it. Click the row — did the price already run (you're late) or is it flat (you're early)?
  5. Alerts tab before games start — movers, clusters, and edges accumulate there so you don't have to watch the tables.

3 · Markets tab

Today's top picks (top of the tab)

Up to five markets ranked by a transparent signal score: book edge (weighted heaviest, up to 12 pts) + net whale flow (up to 5 pts) + a bonus when both agree (+3) − penalties for thin books or a negative book edge. Each card shows exactly which chips earned its score — nothing is a black box.

  • A market only qualifies with a real directional signal: book edge ≥ 1¢ or net whale flow ≥ $10k on one side. No signal, no pick.
  • The list never pads. "Only 2 qualify right now" means exactly that — a quiet slate is information, not a malfunction. Tout services invent five picks daily; this doesn't.
  • The strongest cards show edge + whales agree — the confluence case from section 8. A whale-flow-only card with a low score is one large trader's opinion, not a system signal.
  • Click a card to open its chart. Picks refresh with the whale feed (every 30s).

The top ~40 sports markets by 24-hour volume. Columns: price of the first-listed outcome (in ¢ = implied probability), 1h/24h moves, bid–ask spread, volume, end date.

4 · Edge tab — the prediction layer

The model: sportsbook moneylines (DraftKings via ESPN's free feed) → implied probability → de-vig (strip the book's margin so both sides sum to 100%) → compare to what Polymarket charges you.

ColumnReading it
Buy sideThe outcome with the better (least bad) edge
Poly askWhat it actually costs to buy that side right now
Fair (book)The de-vigged sportsbook probability
EdgeFair − ask. +3.0¢ = books think it's worth 3¢ more than Polymarket charges
EV / $100Expected profit per $100 staked if the book number is the true probability
  • Set "Highlight edge ≥" to your threshold (default 2¢; below ~2¢ is noise + fees).
  • O/U markets only match when the book total and the Polymarket line are identical — a 9.0 total is never compared against an 8.5 market.
  • An empty table above threshold is the tool working, not failing. Edges appear mostly in the hour before game time and get arbed away in minutes.

Optional: multi-book consensus + tennis

1Get a free key at the-odds-api.com (500 requests/month — plenty).
2Paste it into the key field on the Edge tab and hit Connect. Source column switches from "DraftKings" to "N books", and tennis matches get priced (ESPN doesn't carry tennis odds).
3The key lives only in the page's memory — re-paste it after a reload. Reconnect manually (it doesn't auto-poll, to protect your monthly quota).

5 · Whale Feed tab

  • Consensus table (top): net flow per outcome — buys minus sells in dollars across all loaded large trades. +$107k on "Over" means whales are net long the Over by $107k. The trade count matters: +$60k across 8 trades is conviction; one $60k trade is one person's opinion.
  • Feed (bottom): every trade over your minimum — time, trader (click the name → their full Polymarket profile and P&L history), BUY/SELL, price, notional.
  • Drop the min to $5k on quiet mornings, raise to $25k+ during big slates. Untick "Sports only" to see whale money in politics/crypto too.
Whales are on both sides of most games — today's snapshot had big money buying and selling Norrie in the same hour. The signal is sustained one-sided flow, not any single print.

6 · Charts tab

  • Pick a market from the dropdown — both outcomes are listed, so you can chart either side.
  • Ranges: 1H 6H 1D 1W 1M. Hover for the crosshair readout.
  • ● Orange dots = whale trades, placed on the price line when they happened. Hover one for side/size/price. Dots before a run-up = whales led the move; dots after = they chased it.

8 · Discipline (read once, remember always)

An edge is a hypothesis, not a payday. A +4¢ edge vs. DraftKings can mean Polymarket is wrong — or that the book is stale and about to move. Polymarket sometimes leads the books, especially near game time.
  • Best signal = confluence: book edge + same-direction whale flow + a chart that hasn't already run.
  • Respect the spread. A 3¢ edge on a THIN market with a 3¢ spread is zero edge.
  • Small, boring sizing. Even real edges lose ~40% of the time at these prices. No alert here is a reason to bet more than you'd calmly lose.
  • This tool shows you what markets, whales, and books believe. It does not know who wins tonight. Nothing in it is financial advice.
