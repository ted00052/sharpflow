# SharpFlow launch & distribution kit

The audience is prediction-market X. The strategy is not "promote a tool" —
it's **post the output daily, with receipts, and let the tool ride along.**
Nobody shares a link to a dashboard; they share a screenshot that made them
feel like they're missing something.

Replace `SITE` with your URL everywhere below.

---

## Launch thread (post once, pin it)

> **1/** I built a free tool that watches Polymarket sports markets the way a
> sharp would: whale flow, book-vs-market divergence, and price alerts. No
> signup, no token, no paywall. SITE
>
> **2/** The part I care about most: every signal it fires is logged
> automatically to a public git repo and scored against final Polymarket
> prices. Hit rate and ROI are published whether they're good or bad —
> back-filling is impossible. SITE/record.html
>
> **3/** What it does NOT do: guarantee profits, sell picks, or pretend a
> model knows who wins tonight. It shows you where big money is moving and
> where Polymarket disagrees with the sportsbooks. What you do with that is
> on you.
>
> **4/** Built on Polymarket's public APIs + de-vigged DraftKings lines —
> the same fair-odds benchmark Sportstensor scores its own models against.
> Free because the interesting question is whether the record holds up.
> Follow along. 📊

## Daily post template (the actual engine — 2 min/day)

Screenshot the Whale Feed consensus table or a Top Picks card, then:

> 🐋 $XXX,XXX net whale flow on [OUTCOME] ([MARKET]) in the last N hours.
> Book fair: XX¢ · Polymarket: XX¢.
> Live tracker (free): SITE

Rules that keep it credible:
- Post the flow/edge, **never** "bet this."
- When a logged signal loses, post that too — losses are what make the wins
  believable. "Yesterday's signal lost. Record now X–Y (SITE/record.html)."
- Weekly recap post: record, hit rate vs. break-even, best + worst call.

## Channels (in order of value)

1. **X** — daily cadence above; reply under big Polymarket-market threads
   with your relevant chart/flow screenshot (context, not spam).
2. **Polymarket community Discords + r/Polymarket** — share the *record page*
   when relevant to a discussion; mods and users are hostile to tool-shilling
   but receptive to receipts.
3. **Product Hunt** — one launch day; expect a spike then silence. Do it
   after 2–3 weeks of logged record so the page isn't empty.
4. **SEO (slow burn)** — a page comparing yourself honestly to AlphaScope /
   BillyBets ("what SharpFlow doesn't do") ranks for the same searches
   AlphaScope built its funnel on.

## What to expect (so you don't quit at the wrong time)

- Weeks 1–4: single-digit engagement. Normal. The record page is empty-ish
  and that's fine — consistency is the signal.
- The account grows on **resolved calls with receipts**, not on features.
  One screenshot of "logged at 36¢ three days ago, resolved WON, here's the
  commit" outperforms a month of feature posts.
- If after ~100 resolved signals the record is under break-even, say so
  publicly and reposition the tool as flow-tracking only. Honesty about a
  losing edge model is rarer than a winning one — and it keeps the audience.

## Legal hygiene

- Keep "informational only, not financial advice" in the bio and site footer
  (already in the pages).
- Never post guaranteed-profit language, never DM picks, never take money
  for picks — that's the line where regulatory risk starts.
- Check the name "SharpFlow" for trademark conflicts before buying merch or
  a serious domain. Renaming is one find-and-replace today; later it isn't.
